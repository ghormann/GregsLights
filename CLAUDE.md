# GregsLights

C++ (C++0x, pthreads) program that drives our Christmas display: the countdown clock, the sign below it, the garage sign, the large grid and the snowmen. Each runs in its own thread (`GregsDisplay::run()`), and MQTT (`GregMQTT`) brings in names, snowman votes and power data. The source is in `GregsLights/`.

## Building

- From `GregsLights/`: `make -f GregsLights.cbp.mak <target>`. `Makefile` is an identical copy, and both are generated by `cbp2make` from `GregsLights.cbp`, so add new source files to the `.cbp`.
- There are three targets, each with its own object directory:
  - `release`: `obj/Release` → `bin/Release/GregsLights`
  - `debug`: `obj/Debug`
  - `graphics`: `.objs` → `./GregsLights`. This is the wx/GL build with an on-screen preview (`mainFrame.cpp`), and it's the one normally run.
- **The makefile has no header dependencies.** After changing any header, run `make -f GregsLights.cbp.mak clean` and rebuild. Otherwise stale objects keep the old class layout and the program dies at startup with heap corruption (`malloc(): corrupted top size`).
- Check that changes build with no warnings in all three targets (`all`).

## Running

- **Ask before running any GregsLights binary.** It reads `~/greglights_config.json`, connects to the real MQTT broker and sends to the real light controllers.
- Flags: `-t` skips the time check (runs outside show hours), `-d` is debug/test mode, `-y` is New Year mode, `-l` is plain console output.
- `GREGSLIGHTS_SNOWMAN="The Grinch" ./GregsLights -t` keeps one snowman character up and does its signature move about every other move.
- To check visuals without the network, write a scratch program that links the object files and builds `Snowmen`/`GarageSign` against `E131Network("127.0.0.1", …)` and `DDPOutput("127.0.0.1", …)` objects. These only open a socket; nothing is sent unless a `NetworkCollection` thread is attached. Read the pixels back with `getPixal(x,y)->getRed()` (0–100) and turn them into images or GIFs. Use `#define private public` in the scratch file to reach the internals.

## Colors and text

- `RGBColor` and bulb values are percentages (0–100). Pictures load as 0–255 and are passed straight through, so any channel above 100 gets clipped to full brightness. This is existing behavior.
- Black (0,0,0) is "off". Dark colors barely show on LEDs, so use bright ones.
- Sign big font (`drawLetter`/`writeText`/`scrollText`): about 10px per character, and the sign is 96px wide, so about 9 characters fit statically. Unknown characters draw as `?`. It has digits, `, . ! - : '` and `$ # ^ \ +` (special glyphs).
- The small sign font (`writeTextSmall`) has **no digits**.
- `writeTextNew` (TrueType, Liberation Sans) is used on the garage sign and the sky grid. Measure with `GenericGrid::getTextWidthNew()` to center or fit text.
- The sky grid is only 24px tall: one line at font 12, or two lines at font 10 (y = 2 and 13).

## Snowmen yard geometry

Grids from left to right: left snowman (47×95), left splash (24×48), sky (96×24), right splash (24×48), right snowman (47×95).

- Snowman grids have **1"** light spacing. Splash and sky grids have **2"**, so a splash grid is the same physical size as a snowman grid. Anything drawn across grids must be scaled per grid.
- The sky hangs **`SNOWMAN_SKY_RAISE` (16") higher** than the other grids. This was worked out from the old ball paths and could be off. There are no lights below the sky between the splash grids, so anything crossing a splash/sky edge is only fully visible between r and 32−r inches down from the top of the other grids (r is its radius).
- Use the yard helpers in `Snowmen.cpp` (they work in inches) instead of per-grid coordinates:
  - `getYardAreas()`, `drawPictureInYard()`, `drawBallInYard()`, `eraseBallInYard()`
  - `ballFlight()`: every snowball and the cannon ball, at a constant 2"/frame, along a spline that crosses the sky edges inside the visible band. It also handles ducking and eating reactions and the arm follow-through.
- `SnowmenGrid::getPixal()` has **no bounds check**, and neither does `GenericGrid::setBackground(color, x0, y0, x1, y1)`. `drawCircle`, `plotLineWidth`, `showPictureNow` and `Snowmen::fillBox` clip safely.

## Snowman characters

- Characters are `SnowmanPicture` entries in `createSnowmanPictures()`. **Vote IDs are list positions:** add new characters at the end and never insert or remove in the middle. Then tell the user the new ID for the voting site.
- Picture characters (`pic` set) can't throw, so `do_it_snowmen()` limits their moves. To add or fix one, use the `adding-snowman-characters` skill.
- Drawn characters (`pic == NULL`, `rig` set) are drawn in code in `drawRig()` and get every move. They must follow the snowman's layout:
  - head at about y 20–40, facing left, top of head at about y 20 (where the hat sits, at 7,−2)
  - throwing shoulder at (28,42)
  - stuck snowballs at y 29, moved with `nose_ball_dx`
  - mouth set with `mouth_offset_*`
  - for ducking, the head drops 3× the offset, the middle 2× and the bottom 1×

  Keep `show_misses` on for drawn characters.
- **Redraws must give the same result every time.** Some moves redraw a character without clearing the grid first. Anything clock-driven (wagging tails, swinging legs) may only move while a flag such as `flying` or `running` is set, during moves that clear every frame. Otherwise the old position stays lit (Tails once showed three tails).
- Personality fields: `catchphrase` (sky text when the character arrives), `signature` + `signatureText` (`signatureMove()`, guaranteed by move `SNOWMAN_SIGNATURE_BY_MOVE`, then 1 in `SNOWMAN_SIGNATURE_CHANCE`) and `speed`.
- Move counting: only real character moves count toward `SNOWMAN_STEPS` (or `SNOWMAN_STEPS_VOTE_WAITING` while a vote is waiting). A random cannon shot can't replace a character before `SNOWMAN_MIN_MOVES`.

## Clock milestones

`TimeInfo::isMilestone()` marks round numbers, repeated digits and digit runs. The sign, garage sign and clock all build up to and celebrate these (`MILESTONE_CELEBRATE_SECONDS`). Milestones are skipped in no-show mode.

## Working with the user

- Keep changes looking like the surrounding code: C-style, `RGBColor *` statics, `sprintf(message, …)` status strings.
- For visual work, show previews: GIFs, or contact sheets drawn at the real physical layout with 2" lights as 2×2 blocks and the sky raised.
- The profanity filter for texted names is handled outside this repo.
